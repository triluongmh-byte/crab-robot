# Side-entry USB-C

The native J1 repair retains all GPIOs and separate power rails. J1 is at native X21.45/Y−28, rotation 180 degrees, opening toward board-local +Y. The final case check accepts the ordinary 12.5 × 7 × 25 mm plug envelope; the cable route assumes Ø4.5 mm. See the English [wiring guide](WIRING.md#integrated-board), [build validation](../BUILD-VALIDATION.md) and [physical checklist](../case/CHECKLIST-RAP.md). Real USB enumeration and flashing in both plug orientations remain first-board tests.
