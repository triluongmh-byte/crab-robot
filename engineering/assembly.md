# Assembly and provenance
There is no assembly operation: J1–J4 are copper solder landing features, MH1/MH2 are drilled substrate. All six are explicitly excluded from BOM and position export; the schematic agrees. No components, MPNs, supplier identities, stock levels or factory rotation corrections are required or claimed. Empty factory/manual BOMs and CPL are intentional, never a substitute for robot wiring.

Local SolderPad_2x3mm geometry is authored in tools/board/build_pcb.py: single rounded rectangle 2 × 3 mm, 20% corner ratio, F.Cu/F.Mask only, no paste. MountingHole_3.2mm_M3 is copied from the installed KiCad 10.0.5 footprint library into design/Dummy.pretty; its front silkscreen circle stroke is raised from 0.12 to 0.15 mm for packet legibility. Pinless mounting-hole and passive pad symbols are copied and flattened by kicadpy.author from KiCad's bundled Mechanical and Connector_Generic symbol libraries (KiCad library license CC-BY-SA-4.0 with library exception). No downloaded part models are used.

Native default ignored checks are recorded exactly in manufacturing.json: missing_courtyard (integral copper has no fitted body), track_not_centered_on_via (no vias), tuning_profile_track_geometries (no tuned pairs), footprint_filters_mismatch and footprint_type_mismatch (integral pads and pinless mechanical holes); ERC single_global_label (each dummy label occurs twice), four_way_junction (none), simulation_model_issue (no simulation), footprint_filter (local pad/hole names). These are the tool's untouched defaults; no electrical or geometric rule was disabled, widened or overridden. All other findings must be zero.
