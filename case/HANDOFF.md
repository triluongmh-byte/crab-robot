# Open-frame source handoff

The two A1V4-r5 structural frames replace the old body; eight frozen r1 leg parts are reused and both clamp bars are retired. Direct hip-tab pilots, staged PCB insertion, shifted LCD mount (180-degree orientation retained), ordinary side-entry USB and alternative lead routes are in the CAD generators.

See [assembly](README.md), [BOM](BOM.md), [physical checklist](CHECKLIST-RAP.md), [owner inventory](PRINTED-INVENTORY.md) and [deviations](DEVIATIONS.md). The build regenerates current geometry, hash-bound checks, offline plates and the eight-step overview. Exact printed-body identity is unknown; no physical fit, strength or walking result is inferred from the owner photos.
